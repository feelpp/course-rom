Session 12 — EnKF with nonlinear forecasts

Two-hour cours intégré · 60 minutes of practical activity within the 120-minute session

1. Prepare and learning outcomes

Review the preceding session. Read the core notes through the algorithm and its assumptions; further reading is optional. You will connect EnKF, nonlinear-model, ensemble, covariance and use the practical’s checks to interpret the method’s limits. The supplied baseline is independent of unfinished work in previous notebooks.

2. During the session

Minutes Activity

0–15

Recall the motivating problem, prerequisites and assumptions.

15–35

Derive the central equations and trace a small example.

35–60

Practical: baseline, first task and numerical checks.

60–80

Discuss the algorithm, stability or covariance assumptions and expected diagnostics.

80–110

Practical: parameter experiment and held-out comparison.

110–120

Checkpoint: five minutes to finish the evidence, five minutes for discussion.

Open the executable practical and notebook download. The practical’s timed tasks total 60 minutes and fit the two work periods plus the closing checkpoint. Optional extensions are not required within the contact-time budget.

3. Evidence to retain

Keep the executed baseline, the practical’s requested plot/table, your changed experiment and a short explanation of assumptions. Compare methods on the same declared inputs; record validation/test separation where relevant.

4. After class

Use the associated exercises and homework. Submission dates and marking arrangements are announced by the instructor.
