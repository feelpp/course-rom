The stationary practical contains 60 minutes of activities; finish its final checks after class if needed. The thermal prediction practical contains 75 minutes; part B on observations is reserved for the later EnKF session. Full-order solvers and plotting helpers are supplied; students complete the adaptive loops. Notebooks are generated from these same pages and downloaded from their toolbars.

== Results to retain

* Error against dimension, on fixed training and validation sets, for one and two stationary parameters.
* Selected parameter pairs and pointwise error/certificate sweeps: diffusivity alone, then a declared paired path.
* Final certificate and stopping status; maximum rank is not a successful tolerance test.
* Transient error against dimension, selected trajectories, and a new-control output comparison.
* Construction-only full solves/trajectories, separated from reference solves used for diagnostics.

The stationary energy error and nested POD projection error have monotonicity properties. Transient reduced-solve error and conservative certificates must be measured separately; no claim of monotonicity is made for every curve.
