== Practical: model, output and observations

The notebook adapts the supplied 1D reaction–diffusion solver from the tiny RB lab:

[stem]
++++
-\mu u''+u=1,\qquad u(0)=u(1)=0,\qquad \mu\in[0.1,10].
++++

. Run the supplied cells from the top. Locate the parameter, state vector, mean output and sensor indices.
. Change `mu_trial` to at least two values in the allowed range. Describe how the profile and mean change.
. Compare the model's sensor predictions with the synthetic observations. Distinguish a parameter mismatch from observation noise.
. Change the noise level or sensor locations and rerun the observation cells. Explain what information is lost or gained.

Save *one state-profile plot*, *one model/observation plot*, and short answers to the notebook questions.
No reduced solver or assimilation algorithm is required in this opening activity.
Those methods will provide tools to address the needs you identify today.
