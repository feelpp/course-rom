= Session 12 — EnKF with nonlinear forecasts
:navtitle: 12 · EnKF with nonlinear forecasts

*Two-hour cours intégré · 60 minutes of practical activity within the 120-minute session*

== Prepare and learning outcomes

Review xref:sessions/11-kalman.adoc[the preceding session].
Read xref:rom::assimilation/enkf.adoc[the core notes] through the algorithm and its assumptions; further reading is optional.
You will connect EnKF, nonlinear-model, ensemble, covariance and use the practical's checks to interpret the method's limits.
The supplied baseline is independent of unfinished work in previous notebooks.

== During the session

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Minutes |Activity
|0–15 |Recall the motivating problem, prerequisites and assumptions.
|15–35 |Derive the central equations and trace a small example.
|35–60 |Practical: baseline, first task and numerical checks.
|60–80 |Discuss the algorithm, stability or covariance assumptions and expected diagnostics.
|80–110 |Practical: parameter experiment and held-out comparison.
|110–120 |Checkpoint: five minutes to finish the evidence, five minutes for discussion.
|===

Open xref:labs/session12-enkf.adoc[the executable practical and notebook download].
The practical's timed tasks total 60 minutes and fit the two work periods plus the closing checkpoint.
Optional extensions are not required within the contact-time budget.

== Evidence to retain

Keep the executed baseline, the practical's requested plot/table, your changed experiment and a short explanation of assumptions.
Compare methods on the same declared inputs; record validation/test separation where relevant.
