= Course map — 14 integrated sessions
:navtitle: Course map

*M2 ROM & Data-Driven ROM · 28 contact hours · Cours intégré*

The course introduces digital twins and develops numerical methods that can support their prediction and update processes.
Lectures, short derivations, student notebook work and discussion share the same contact-time budget.
*Data assimilation is mandatory.*

== Prerequisites

Basic numerical linear algebra, PDE discretization, Python/NumPy, and elementary probability.
The first experiment supplies the full-order solver; no Feel++ compilation is needed.

== Learning outcomes

* Explain the roles of models, observations and assimilation in a digital-twin scenario.
* Construct and compare reduced spaces using POD and greedy selection.
* Interpret a residual-based error estimate and its assumptions.
* Explain and measure what EIM/DEIM changes in online evaluation.
* Reconstruct states from observations using GEIM/PBDW and examine stability/noise.
* Use KF/EnKF to combine forecasts and sequential observations, including ROM forecasts.

Each session links a runnable practical, with supplied setup, timed student tasks and a submission checkpoint.
The final thermal-fin practicals also provide a downloadable data file.
Aim for approximately half of the total contact time in student practical activity, with the remainder devoted to explanations and discussion.

== Course readings and reference material

The xref:rom::index.adoc[reference library] has a broader scope than this course.
Session pages identify required sections; other chapters and historical PDFs are not an implied additional reading list.
SCM, extensive convergence proofs, geometric transformations, nonlinear certification, other filtering variants and learned surrogate models can be explored outside the selected route.

== Practical work and assessment

For each practical, retain the notebook, the requested plots and short explanations of assumptions and results.
Synthetic truth is available for evaluating experiments; it must not become an undeclared input to an estimation algorithm.
Specific submissions and marking rules will be announced by the instructor.
The xref:archive.adoc[archive] contains previous offerings, including deadlines that no longer apply.
