== A cours intégré

We have *14 sessions of 2 hours*, combining mathematical explanation, guided computation and discussion.
You will construct reduced approximations, examine their errors and costs, and combine models with observations.
*Data assimilation is a required part of the course.*

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Sessions |Course progression
|1–4 |Digital-twin motivation; RB, a posteriori estimation, greedy and POD.
|5–7 |Non-affine problems; EIM and DEIM.
|8–10 |General measurements, GEIM and PBDW.
|11–13 |Sequential assimilation: KF, EnKF and ROM coupling.
|14 |Integrated experiment and synthesis.
|===

See the xref:syllabus.adoc[course map and learning outcomes].
Detailed session pages and practicals are released as they become available; only session 1 is part of this opening release.
