= Exercises and homework — EIM and DEIM
:stem: latexmath

For sessions 5–7. Suggested work outside class: 90 minutes.
Use the released practicals as runnable starting points. Submit one notebook and at most two pages of interpretation.
Dates and marking rules are announced by the instructor; no historical deadline applies.

== A. Interpolation identities (25 minutes)

. Starting from the EIM residual normalization, prove that its interpolation matrix is lower triangular with unit diagonal in exact arithmetic.
. Show that the DEIM operator reproduces its source-basis span and interpolates at selected coordinates.
. State the assumptions of the Euclidean DEIM error bound. Explain why the condition number alone is not the amplification factor in that bound.

Use xref:rom::hyper-reduction/index.adoc[EIM] and xref:rom::hyper-reduction/deim.adoc[DEIM] for notation.

== B. A held-out rank study (35 minutes)

Use xref:labs/session05-eim.adoc[session 5] and xref:labs/session06-deim.adoc[session 6].
Freeze the Gaussian training collection and test on at least five other parameter pairs.
Compare ranks 4, 8 and 12 with the same samples and metric.
Report training maximum interpolation error, held-out maximum error, POD projection error and interpolation inverse norm.
Include one deliberately narrower source and label it as outside the training width range.
Explain why improved training accuracy does not establish a uniform parameter-domain guarantee.

== C. The online contract (30 minutes)

Use xref:labs/session07-hyper-reduction.adoc[session 7].
Instrument the number of source coordinates evaluated by each query.
Report full/reduced scalar outputs, absolute errors, median repeated timings and dimensions.
List all offline work and explain which extra arrays would be needed for a full-field output.
A measured slowdown is acceptable; an unexplained comparison of different outputs is not.

== Submission checklist

* Fresh-kernel notebook execution, training/test parameters and random seeds.
* One rank/error table, one illustrative field plot and one timing table.
* Derivations and a clear distinction between source interpolation error and reduced PDE error.

Optional extension: derive a bound for the total error when only the forcing is interpolated and the original operator remains coercive.
