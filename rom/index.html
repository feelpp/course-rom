Reduced Models & Digital Twins

A growing reference on reduced approximation, efficient simulation and assimilation of observations into numerical models.

Available native notes

  • Digital twins: connecting models and observations — motivation, a running thermal example, and the roles of reduction and assimilation.

Learn through a course

The M2 ROM & Data-Driven ROM course provides a 14-session route with lectures and practical work. Its opening session links the introductory notes to a runnable experiment.

Scope of the broader material

The source collection extends beyond this course: linear algebra and functional analysis, POD and approximation theory, reduced bases and certification, geometry, transient and nonlinear problems, hyper-reduction, data assimilation, applications and Feel++ implementation.

Native web conversion is in progress. The introduction above is the first available unit; the other subjects are planned reference areas, not additional required M2 readings. Selected existing PDFs remain accessible through the teaching archive.

Course pages select the material needed for a teaching session. Reference pages provide the reusable explanations; advanced theory will remain available independently of any one course’s schedule.
