Non-affinity and empirical interpolation

An affine decomposition separates parameter dependence from spatial operators:

\[A(\mu)=\sum_{q=1}^{Q_a}\theta_q(\mu)A_q, \qquad f(\mu)=\sum_{q=1}^{Q_f}\eta_q(\mu)f_q.\]

For small fixed numbers of terms, projected matrices and vectors are assembled offline. A moving Gaussian source or a nonlinear constitutive law generally lacks such a small exact decomposition. Evaluating the entire source or nonlinear residual online can then dominate the reduced solve. Empirical interpolation constructs a separable approximation whose coefficients are determined from selected evaluations.

EIM: build the basis and interpolation points together

For a finite training collection of scalar fields, start with an empty interpolant. At step \(m\), find a training field with maximum infinity-norm interpolation residual, then a grid point where that residual is largest in magnitude. Divide the residual by its signed value at that point to obtain the next basis function. Preserve previous points and functions.

\[I_m f=Q_m c,\qquad (P_m^TQ_m)c=P_m^Tf.\]

Here columns of \(P_m\) are coordinate vectors selecting the points. The residual vanishes at previously selected points, so the interpolation matrix is lower triangular with unit diagonal in exact arithmetic. This is an algebraic interpolation property; it does not imply that the matrix is well conditioned. Stop when the maximum training residual meets tolerance or becomes numerically negligible.

Error relative to best approximation

For a bounded interpolation operator that is the identity on its range:

\[\|f-I_m f\|_\infty\leq (1+\|I_m\|_{\infty\to\infty}) \inf_{v\in\operatorname{range}(Q_m)}\|f-v\|_\infty.\]

For any such \(v\), write the error as \((I-I_m)(f-v)\) and use the operator norm. The discrete norm concerns this grid; a continuum claim needs control between grid points.

Coefficient approximation and PDE error

Interpolating a PDE coefficient creates an additional consistency error, separate from Galerkin truncation. For a right-hand-side approximation with a fixed coercive operator, the full solution perturbation satisfies:

\[\|u_h-\widetilde u_h\|_V\leq \frac{\|f-\widetilde f\|_{V'}}{\alpha_{\mathrm{LB}}}.\]

For an interpolated operator, positivity/coercivity can be lost. Check stability of the approximated operator and include its consistency residual; the certificate for the original operator is not automatically unchanged. The course practical starts with a non-affine source to isolate interpolation from this additional difficulty.
