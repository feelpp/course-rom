DEIM: POD-based empirical interpolation

Read non-affinity and EIM first. DEIM constructs a POD basis of source or nonlinear-term snapshots, then chooses interpolation coordinates for that basis. The state basis and nonlinear-term basis have different roles and generally different dimensions.

Construction

Let the columns of \(U_m\) be Euclidean-orthonormal POD modes of the term to approximate. Choose the largest-magnitude entry of the first mode. For each subsequent mode, interpolate it using previous modes and points, then choose the largest-magnitude residual entry. The new point cannot repeat an earlier one while the residual is nonzero.

\[D_m f=U_m(P_m^TU_m)^{-1}P_m^Tf.\]

Use a linear solve; the inverse in the formula is not an instruction to compute an explicit inverse in code. Interpolation is exact at the selected coordinates and on the span of the retained modes.

Euclidean error bound

For orthonormal \(U_m\), distinct selected coordinates and nonsingular interpolation matrix:

\[\|f-D_m f\|_2\leq \|(P_m^TU_m)^{-1}\|_2\, \|(I-U_mU_m^T)f\|_2.\]

For a proper nonzero oblique projection, its norm and that of its complement coincide. Apply this fact to the orthogonal projection residual, since DEIM reproduces the retained span. The full-rank case is exact. The bound uses the inverse norm, not just a condition number or the snapshot singular values.

A small POD training tail does not by itself guarantee a small test error or a well-conditioned sampling matrix. Record the held-out projection error, interpolation error and inverse norm separately. For a weighted state metric, first specify which space and norm the nonlinear snapshots inhabit; the Euclidean bound cannot simply be relabeled as a mass-norm bound.

Nonlinear reduced systems

With a state basis \(Z_r\), precompute the reduced lifting operator:

\[Z_r^T f(Z_r a;\mu)\approx \underbrace{Z_r^TU_m(P_m^TU_m)^{-1}}_{C_{rm}} P_m^T f(Z_r a;\mu).\]

Efficient selected evaluation depends on the model. For pointwise nonlinearities, selected state entries can use precomputed rows of the state basis. For a discretized PDE stencil, neighboring degrees of freedom are also needed. A global integral or a black-box routine returning a full vector may prevent the assumed saving.

Practice and reading

Run the interpolation/bound experiment, then the reduced online source experiment.
