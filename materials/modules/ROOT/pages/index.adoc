= Reduced Models & Digital Twins
:navtitle: Reference library
:!numbered:

[.lead]
A growing reference on reduced approximation, efficient simulation and assimilation of observations into numerical models.

== Available native notes

* xref:digital-twins.adoc[Digital twins: connecting models and observations] — motivation, a running thermal example, and the roles of reduction and assimilation.

* xref:notation.adoc[Notation and mathematical conventions] — state spaces, norms, projection, residuals and observations.

* xref:foundations/riesz.adoc[Hilbert spaces, dual norms and Riesz representation] — definitions, a matrix solve and connections to residuals and sensors.

* xref:reduction/galerkin.adoc[Galerkin reduced models] and xref:reduction/pod.adoc[POD] — equations, snapshot spaces and offline/online computations.
* xref:certification/residual-bound.adoc[Residual bounds and effectivity] — assumptions, derivation, worked effectivity and a xref:certification/residual-bound.adoc#bounds-summary[summary of bounds]. Efficient estimator evaluation is advanced reading.
* xref:certification/coercivity-lower-bound.adoc[Coercivity lower bounds] and xref:certification/residual-bound.adoc[residual certificates] — the min-Theta construction, its assumptions, effectivity and the residual norm.
* xref:certification/extensions.adoc[General outputs, nonsymmetric operators and time dependence] — a concise extension guide, main guarantees, affine online prediction and the transient 2D thermal/POD route.

* xref:reduction/greedy.adoc[Greedy selection] and xref:hyper-reduction/index.adoc[EIM/DEIM] — space selection, interpolation and online cost.
* xref:assimilation/geim.adoc[GEIM] and xref:assimilation/variational.adoc[PBDW] — sensor functionals, stability, noise and model discrepancy.
* xref:assimilation/kalman.adoc[KF] and xref:assimilation/enkf.adoc[EnKF] — sequential observations and reduced forecasts.
* xref:applications/thermal-fin.adoc[Thermal-fin application] — mass POD, sparse time stepping and assimilation.

== Learn through a course

The xref:course-rom::index.adoc[M2 ROM & Data-Driven ROM course] provides a 14-session route with lectures and practical work.
Its xref:course-rom::sessions/01-digital-twins.adoc[opening session] links the introductory notes to a runnable experiment.

== Scope of the broader material

The source collection extends beyond this course: linear algebra and functional analysis, POD and approximation theory, reduced bases and certification, geometry, transient and nonlinear problems, hyper-reduction, data assimilation, applications and Feel++ implementation.

Native web conversion is in progress.
The selected M2 route has native notes and practicals throughout.
Broader subjects such as SCM, geometry and nonlinear certification remain planned reference areas, not additional required M2 readings.
Selected existing PDFs remain accessible through the xref:course-rom::archive.adoc[teaching archive].

Course pages select the material needed for a teaching session.
Reference pages provide the reusable explanations; advanced theory will remain available independently of any one course's schedule.
